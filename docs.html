How to use Calculators

Every calculator in SignOS 4.x follows a specific 3-step validation protocol. Follow this to ensure $0 margin leakage.

  • 1
    Define Physical Specs

    Input width, height, and quantity. The system calculates yield (boards/LF) instantly.

  • 2
    Review In-House Costs

    Switch to Tab 2 to see machine time and ink cost. If profit is red, adjust price.

  • 3
    Perform Vended Check

    Check Tab 3. If outsource price is lower than shop cost, prioritize vended.

Backend Logic Hierarchy

Level 1: Master Data

The "Brain." Contains raw costs (e.g., $0.65 Coro Blank) and Market Curves (Blue Sheet).

Level 1.5: The Matrix

The "Intercept." Allows Admins to toggle materials ON/OFF globally without breaking formulas.

Level 2: Product Logic

The "Context." Calculates specific item costs (e.g., CNC Time for ACM vs Shear Time).
