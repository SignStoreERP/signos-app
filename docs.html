Common Terminology

Rigid Substrate Logic ▼
  • ACM (Aluminum Composite): Durable metal exterior for long-term outdoor use.
  • Shear Cut: Standard straight-line cutting (included in base price).
  • CNC Routing: Required for circles, logos, or custom shapes. Adds a flat setup fee ($30-$50).
  • Single-Sided (SS) vs Double-Sided (DS): Rigid DS typically adds 50% to the base price, not 100%, because the material is already paid for.
Vinyl & Roll Media Logic ▼
  • Calendered (Standard): For flat signs and wall decals (Intermediate).
  • Cast (Premium): Required for Vehicle Wraps and high-durability needs (751/IJ180).
  • Simple Weeding: Circles, squares, large text (2 mins/sqft labor).
  • Complex Weeding: Small text or logos with many "islands" (8 mins/sqft labor).
  • Hem & Grommet: Banner finishing included in linear foot price.

Quoting Best Practices

1. Select the Material First

The system automatically calculates machine speeds based on material density. Don't select ACM if you are quoting Coroplast just to see the price difference; the labor math will be wrong.

2. Respect the Loss Alert

If the system shows a red pulse, your markup is too low. Increasing quantity often solves this by reducing the "Labor Burden" per sign.

3. Shipping Verification

For ACM or Rigid signs over 48", the system assumes LTL Freight ($199 flat). Warn clients about freight costs for large rigid boards.

Manufacturing Standards

Material Yields

  • Rigid: 10% Waste Factor (Standard)
  • Vinyl: 15% Waste Factor (Standard)
  • Wraps: 25% Waste Factor (Complex Panels)

Machine Speeds

  • HP R1000: 25 LF/Hr (Draft Mode)
  • HP Latex 300: 150 SqFt/Hr (High Speed)
  • CNC MultiCam: 1-2 Mins/SqFt
