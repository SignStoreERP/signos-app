Terminology Cheat Sheet

ACM

Shear vs CNC

Shear is straight cuts only (Free). CNC is for shapes/circles ($30+ fee).

VIN

Cast vs Calendered

Cast for vehicles/long-term. Calendered for walls/flat signs.

LAB

Simple vs Complex Weeding

Small letters/islands multiply weeding labor from 2 mins to 8 mins/sqft.

Profit Guard Protocol

Red Pulse Alert

If you see a pulsing red bar, you are pricing below shop cost. Increase Qty or Price.

The Vended Check

Always check Tab 3 (Vended). If outsourced cost is cheaper than shop cost, prioritize selling that.
